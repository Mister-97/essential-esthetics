Is Essential Esthetics close to Whitehall?

Very close. Head south on S Hamilton Rd for about 10 minutes. We are at 3494 S Hamilton Rd, Columbus, OH 43232.

Do I need to do anything before my appointment?

About a quarter inch of growth is ideal, roughly 2 to 3 weeks after shaving. Gently exfoliate the day before, arrive clean, and wear loose clothing. That is it.

Is it going to hurt?

Less than you think. Hard wax grips hair, not skin, and Latasia’s technique is built to minimize discomfort. Most first-timers say the same thing: “That was it?”

Is this a judgement-free space?

Always. Every body is welcome here. Ask any question, say anything that makes you more comfortable, and expect zero judgement from start to finish.
